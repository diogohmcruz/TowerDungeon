# Dread is split from Supplies and hunger is staged

Supplies stay the food a party carries, drained as they are today. A separate Dread gauge, per expedition, rises with depth, time and the clock band of the floor draw, in four steps that worsen what floors can bring; it is not food, drains no Supplies, is eased by a Camp or light, and resets at Extraction. Running out of Supplies no longer deals damage at once: units go Hungry (Sanity falls, a Camp cannot heal them), and only after a set time Starving (health damage).

The first version fused food and fear in one drain, to simulate the dread of spending what the party needs. The book keeps them apart: rations are discrete and missing them gates recovery before it harms, while the torch clock repeats and never marks off (pp. 95-96, 140). Splitting them lets the player read hunger and dread separately and gives a reaction window before units die.

## Consequences

- Salt, Porters, Carrying Capacity and Loadout salting are unchanged because Supplies are unchanged.
- Sanity and Starvation rules change, and the Year's Quarter effects need re-checking against Dread.
- Tuning is a new surface: the Dread steps and the Hungry-to-Starving time are config numbers.
