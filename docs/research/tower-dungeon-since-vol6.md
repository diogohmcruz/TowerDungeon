# Tower Dungeon: state of the manga through chapter 31

Research snapshot as of **2026-10-10**. Extends `docs/towe_dungeon_manga.md` (written at volume 6, March 2026) and corrects it where sources disagree. Gathered by a research workflow whose reports were each re-checked by an independent skeptical pass.

**Read this first.** No source available to the research (publisher pages, reviews, wikis) describes the *content* of chapters 24-31. Official pages carry titles, dates and short editor teasers; the chapters themselves are paywalled and no scanlations were read. The Fandom wiki's last real chapter page is ch.20 and its summary is still "TBA". Treat anything about ch.25-31 beyond the items marked **[official]** as unknown.

Confidence labels used throughout:

- **[official]**: Kodansha, K MANGA, Monthly Shonen Sirius, the editors' X account, Penguin Random House.
- **[review]**: independent English volume reviews (Anime UK News, Anime Fire, Goodreads).
- **[wiki]**: Fandom wiki. Fan-written, secondary, and sometimes wrong.
- **[fan]**: TV Tropes, blogs, reader comments.
- **[inferred]**: derived from titles or dates.

## 1. Publication status

- Serialised in Monthly Shonen Sirius since 2023-10-26. **Ongoing, no hiatus [official]**. Passed 1 million copies in circulation by Aug 2026 (editor's post, Wikipedia). No anime announced.
- **Latest chapter: ch.31, "The Ancient King and the Undying"**, Sirius Nov 2026 issue, K MANGA 2026-09-24 (JP 09-25). Ch.32 is expected in the Dec 2026 issue, late Oct, unconfirmed.

- English: Kodansha USA, print under Vertical Comics, $13.95. **K MANGA simulpubs chapters monthly (around the 25th) in many regions, so English readers are about two volumes ahead of English print.**

## 3. Corrections to `docs/towe_dungeon_manga.md`

1. Volumes: 7 as of 2026-09-09 (not 6), and the series is at ch.31.
2. **Aridellia** is the K MANGA spelling, not "Ardiellia". Other official-ish spellings: Lilicen, Funapa. Fan spellings vary widely (Aridelia, Lilisen, Eriquo, Karash/Carache, Minservelle/Minsabelle). Pick one canonical spelling per name.
3. **Aridellia is not the main villain.** She is a necromancer *ally* in the party (the necromancer is a species or race). The villain is a different necromancer who took over the king's corpse. Reviews and wiki differ on details, and the villain is still unnamed.
4. **"Giants" is not a race.** The big armoured things are Tower Gatekeepers. **"Fairies"** is really Dorias, a telepathic living-statue "tower fairy". **Beastfolk** are Nyabilians (cat people, e.g. Garamna), Ratwicks (Churafi, Gessi) and the crow-goat Krogi.
5. **Forests, rivers and "organic growth" are unsupported.** What the sources do support: water sources, a waterway monster (floor 14), huge halls, wooden stairs on the outer wall, and ruins.
6. **"Permanent injuries" and "weapon degradation" are unsupported.** Dragon Spores and Panacea heal, and can even reattach limbs.
7. Drakmetamorph spelling is unresolved. Reviews use "Dracomorph". Kodansha USA may use "Dragonmorph". The wiki uses "Dracometamorph".
8. Family relationships are contradictory across sources (is Minservelle the dead king's sister, or are she and Ignelia sisters?). Do not fix any in the design docs yet.

## 4. Tower facts

- **3 km wide [wiki/Mangapedia].** Height is disputed: the wiki says 1800 levels of about 10 m (18 km, but "18 km" is a derived figure), TV Tropes says about 15 km and 1000-plus floors. Safe wording: "3 km wide, 1000-plus floors".
- 12 main pillars run the full height. About 50,000 steps. Each floor has a reference pillar showing its floor number [wiki].
- Royal blood opens some doors, but gatekeepers can lock doors even against it [wiki].
- Dragons circle the tower and maul anything flying up, so the only route is the interior stairs [wiki].
- **A hidden spiral staircase runs from floor 1 to floor 80** [review: Anime UK News vols 1-2, wiki]. The Excavators' Union repaired its damaged top section.
- **Humans who die in the tower become undead** [wiki, plus the Necromancer controlling corpses left on each floor, per reviews].

## 5. Bestiary

Creature details are mostly **[wiki]** unless noted.

- **Chymus Insect, "Giant Slime".** (The game's separate "Slime" is an intentional invention; see the mapping table.) Two heads, precise mucus shots that immobilise, a mucus layer that grants fire immunity, acid and poison. **Salt dissolves the mucus**, and the slime targets whoever carries salt. [review] A shapeshifting slime trapped Lilicen, and Yuva afterwards carries "more salt than water" (hence "Master Salt-Sprinkler"). Killing the first one leaves a glowing orb, Yuva's pendant.- **Tower Gatekeepers.** Big, black-armoured, white-masked, with human-like faces under the helmets. They carry shields, maces and swords, speak a Latin-like ancient language, and at least one fires a harpoon-like projectile from an arm. Per a thought-link vision they hoard corpses to turn into undead. Mask emblem resembles the Safeguard from *Blame!* [fan observation, not stated officially].
- **Basilisks (floors 59-60).** Giant and petrifying. A victim turned to stone is smashed by the basilisk, which lifts the curse. Necro-maggots then infect the remains and add them to the undead army [review: Anime Fire vol 5]. Sargan's mask negates petrification.
- **Upper Body Dragon (floor 80).** A fire-breathing dragon missing its lower half. Ignelia says the Necromancer plans to fully revive it. Eliquo's dragon-slaying arrowhead was the Royal Guard captain's trump card against it.
- **Undead (亡者兵) and necromites.** They come from dead intruders and from necromite larvae.
- **Ruins-Dwellers.** Intelligent, harmless humanoids. One stole the squad's luggage, apologised, returned it with compensation, and a child led them to the floor 1-to-80 staircase. They cook insects with salt.
- **Dragon Spores.** A cure-all mushroom and the Panacea ingredient. A healing resource, not an enemy.
- **Waterway Monster (floor 14).** Almost no detail.
- Wiki species list with no description: Mimics, Stone Golem, Hippogriff, Crowgoats, Primeval People.
- **Elder dragon** (ch.24 title) and the Necromancer's undead hordes (ch.29 teaser) are the only post-vol-6 creatures with any support.

## 6. Characters and factions

- **Neo Squad Seven** (the reformed Seventh Squad): **Yuva** (farm boy, super-strong, "salt-scattering expert", royal Drakmetamorph lineage revealed in vol 3-4, can control weight [wiki]); **Lilicen** (Barbitos fire mage, childhood friend of Eliquo); **Eliquo** (squad leader, best archer, carries the dragon-slaying weapon); **Iscale** (Drakmetamorph, one wing, leads the Anti-Nausea Medicine Alliance); **Ryxul** (Iscale's mute partner); **Aridellia** (necromancer ally); **Sargan** (old fire mage; "brother of Karash" rests on the wiki and one spoiler-tagged vol 7 reader review); **Churafi** and **Gessi** (Ratwicks); **Dorias** (tower fairy).
- **Fortress side:** **Funapa** (Yuva's younger sister [official], of royal blood, gathers people toward a goal), **Garamna** (cat-person Parliament guard), the **Excavation Alliance** (looting guild, gave Yuva the rings), **Miligore** (floating, limbless sorcerer-chancellor of Parliament; pronoun and true form disputed).
- **Royal side:** **Minservelle** (Royal Guard captain, over 100 years old), **Princess Ignelia** (contacted Yuva in dreams, says she cannot be killed until the villain reaches the top [fan]).
- **Villains:** the **Necromancer** (wears the king's body, wants the orb and the revival of the Upper Body Dragon, still unnamed), **Karash** (man-eating Drakmetamorph marquis, necromancer puppet, killed by Lilicen's fire in vol 5 [review]), the **Gatekeepers**.
- **Eastern Land** (new in vol 6-7): "princesses from a far eastern country". Names beyond that are single-source.

## 7. Magic, combat and survival rules

What the sources actually support, versus what the old doc assumed.

- **Casting needs mana storage.** Lilicen's magic is charged through a pendant, and a creature steals it [review: Anime UK News]. "Mana recovers only while sleeping" and Sargan's "batteries" are wiki/blog only.
- **Counters are specific.** Salt beats slime; Sargan's mask negates petrification; dragons, often thought fire-immune, are weak to close-range explosive fire [review: Anime Fire vol 5].
- **Healing is a resource, not time.** Dragon Spores, Panacea and the **Azza-lugh-Novael** relic. The relic is wiki-described as healing, evolving and a one-use resurrection power. It is the Necromancer's real target, since he demands the orb on floor 100 in exchange for Ignelia.
- **Telepathy:** Dorias is telepathic and knows the layout and traps, and the **Thought-Binding Ring** gives linked minds that deepen with trust [official vol 7 blurb]. Its exact effect is unknown.
- **"Magic begets magic":** tower guardians are drawn to Yuva's group [review, cause unclear].
- **Necromancy loop:** a dead intruder rises as undead, and the Necromancer's maggots infect corpses. Aridellia produces necromite larvae too (the wiki says about 1000 a day).
- **Lineage matters:** royal blood opens doors and breaks seals (wiki: Funapa's blood freed Luognam). Yuva and Funapa's royal blood predates ch.20 [review].

## 8. Open mysteries (updated)

- **Answered:** Yuva's lineage, Aridellia's past, Karash's fate.
- **Still open:** the Necromancer's identity, the "sovereign's secret sin" (ch.18), the tower's origin and why it descended, what the Gatekeepers are, the Eastern Land's role, who the Half-Dragon and the "ancient king" are, and what is at the summit.

## 9. Observations that could map to the game

Facts only; deciding what to do with them belongs to the brainstorm.

- **The shortcut feature has a literal source:** the hidden staircase from floor 1 to 80.
- **Dying in the tower turns people into undead.** That is a natural consequence for a wiped party, or for lost reinforcement waves.
- **Salt as a counter-item** against slimes, and Sargan's mask against basilisks: named, specific counters.
- **Gatekeepers are floor-lock guardians** that can override royal-blood locks and hoard corpses.
- **Basilisks (floors 59-60)** convert wiped units into undead via stone, smashing and maggots.
- **Dragon Spores / Panacea** are a healing item, a candidate food-style resource alongside food.
- **Ruins-Dwellers** are a non-hostile trade or lore encounter on a floor.
- **Funapa's gather-people ability and the Excavation Alliance / Parliament guards** match the reinforcement wave and recruitment themes.
- **The Thought-Binding Ring** is a squad-level telepathy buff that grows with trust.
- **The Eastern Land's arrival** is a "second expedition" structure.

### New findings and corrections

- **Half-dragon has been present since ch.2** (title: 半身の竜と火の魔法使い, "The Half-Dragon and the Fire Mage"), so the ch.29 title is a callback, not a new creature. A vol 1 review has the half-body dragon guarding the only staircase of the floor-80 hall. The party retreated and the army halted the rescue [review].
- **Chapter 1 title is 餌撒き名人ユーヴァ ("bait/feed-scattering master")**, since Yuva feeds birds each morning. His nickname **塩撒き名人 ("salt-scattering master")** comes from salting the floor-50 monster. They are two separate things.
- **Floor-50 monster:** the vol 1 review spells it 大縻汁蟲 and a character list spells it 糜汁蟲 (same creature, spelling variant). Mangapedia and reviewers call it a 大スライム or a "slime-type" monster, but no official source does. The game's invented Slime is therefore a loose extrapolation, not canon. Mangapedia is a low-trust source.
- **Necromancer's goal is reported inconsistently.** One fan-wiki entry says he wants to revive the half-dragon on floor 80 (a vol 1 reviewer calls this reader speculation). Another says he will swap the princess for Yuva's orb on floor 100 (from the ch.1 scene). Both are [fan]. The princess tells Yuva not to hold back because she cannot be killed before the top floor.
- **死霊術士 ("necromancer") is a race name**, not a title, and the race is discriminated against [review: Booklive, papy cocolog, 何しよう?]. Aridellia is one of them. The villain is different and unnamed.
- **Lilicen's mana recovers only while she sleeps**, and she stores mana in a **蓄魔器 ("mana-storage device")** that lets her cast remotely [fan list, April 2025, confirmed twice]. Her full name in that list is リリアノレッタ.
- **Rings:** the 思念結びの指輪 has "too many drawbacks" per a reader comment from before vol 5, so it dates from vol 4 or earlier. A vol 6 reviewer says the rings moved the Excavation Alliance (now the **新掘削連合**) from treasure hunting to **necromancer subjugation (死霊術士討伐)**.
- **Eastern Land (vol 6):** a single reviewer names **朔丸 (Sakumaru)** and **砥姫 (Suzuri-hime)** from a Japan-like country. Suzuri-hime is "a giant samurai-like exoskeleton of blood and flesh?" (the question mark is the reviewer's). Booklive readers confirm "completely Japanese-looking people" appear. That is the same single-source status as the earlier wiki claim.
- **Luognam placement is unresolved.** Reader-comment dates on the aggregator site are unreliable (some are free-release or crawl dates), so the earlier hint that the fight ended around ch.25-26, or in ch.23, is dropped.
- **"妹は実は姉だった" (ch.22 reader comment)** may refer to a chief lady-in-waiting named ファンシー, not Funapa. Funapa is Yuva's younger sister per the official blurbs.
- **Floor progress:** a fan site says the party had passed **floor 75 as of April 2026**; ch.30 puts the Necromancer on floor 80 [official teaser].
- **Gatekeeper marks:** black armour with a **統治局 (Governing Bureau)** or **Safeguard** mark on the forehead, a *BLAME!* cross-reference reviewers noticed. It is a reviewer's inference, not stated lore.
- **Tone, per reviewers:** a Wizardry-style hack-and-slash with Soulslike difficulty and FOE-like roaming bosses. Falling is fatal. Herbs and antidotes heal serious organ injuries quickly. **Rival delvers sometimes ambush the party for money.** Smashing pots and chests yields valuables. Weapons wear out (Sargan). Erikuo draws maps by hand.
- **Art, per reviewers:** the "white Nihei" (versus the "black Nihei" of *BLAME!* and *Biomega*), with clean lines, high contrast, large negative space, huge architecture and dialogue-light storytelling.
- **Reception:** Booklive vol 7 rating 4.7. Some readers say the cast is too big, the art has become rough, and the story may end in about two more volumes. These are opinions, not announcements. Nothing official on length.
- **Vol 7 page count conflicts** (160 pages in tonomatsuri, 172 in K MANGA). Not important.
- **Official name spellings seen in Japanese:** エリクォ/エリクオ, リリセン, イグネリア, ミンサーベル, アリデリア, ミリゴア, サルガン, カラーシュ, ガラムナ, イスケイル, リクスル, チュラフィ, ゲッシ, ドリアス, フナパ, クローギー (crow-goat). 竜化人 is written ドラクメタモル.
